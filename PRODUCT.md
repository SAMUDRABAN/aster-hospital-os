# Aster Hospital OS

Approved user brief: a professional, dynamic hospital EHR and complete management/CRM frontend demo. Market-neutral, midsize multispecialty hospital. Synthetic records only. Connected scheduling, encounter, billing, workflow and discharge coordination. Primary users are operations managers; clinicians, front desk and patients have task-specific views. Success means complete, repeatable cross-module interactions with transparent simulation boundaries. No live clinical or payment integration. User approved the plan and calm-precision direction on 14 October 2026.

This is an Operate surface. No marketing landing page. Persistent navigation, an action-oriented Today workspace, an optional contextual drawer and a mobile patient companion. The approved direction takes precedence over randomized concept exploration. Build and publish a private, resettable demo. Browser QA uses gstack /browse with its Playwright driver.
